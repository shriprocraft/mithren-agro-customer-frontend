Isolated products on white, sourced from Wikimedia Commons under **public domain or CC0** only, so no attribution is legally required; it is recorded here anyway. Each was downloaded once as Commons' 1280px rendition and processed locally (backdrop levelled to white, trimmed, centred on a white canvas at ~82% fill; 1000×1000 for products, 1280×800 `-wide` versions for category tiles; JPEG). The products' colours and shadows were not retouched.
